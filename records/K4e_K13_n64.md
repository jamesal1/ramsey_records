## How it was found

A Cayley graph on a group G is given by an inverse-closed connection set S: x ~ y exactly when x⁻¹y is in S. The search makes membership of each inverse pair in S a SAT variable and adds a blocking clause for every forbidden subgraph found in G or its complement, until the solver either returns a valid S or proves none exists.

A sweep over the non-abelian groups of each order (CaDiCaL 1.5.3) found this graph on SmallGroup(64,4) in 1.01 s. Because K14-e contains K13, it also proves R(K4-e, K14-e) ≥ 65.

DS1.18 lists a construction of Jiang, Liang, Sun and Xu (its 3.1(k)) as R(K4-e, K_{m+n+1}) ≥ R(3, m+1) + R(3, n+1) + n. The published paper (Graphs Combin. 37 (2021) 651–661) ends in + m, the smaller index. The misprinted form would give 67 here; the correct form gives only 57.
