## How it was found

A Cayley graph on a group G is given by an inverse-closed connection set S: x ~ y exactly when x⁻¹y is in S. The search makes membership of each inverse pair in S a SAT variable and adds a blocking clause for every forbidden subgraph found in G or its complement, until the solver either returns a valid S or proves none exists.

A sweep over groups of order 116 found this graph on Z4 × Z29 ≅ Z116 (CaDiCaL 1.5.3, 99 s); a longer run on Z2 × Z2 × Z29 (8 min) found the same graph up to isomorphism.
