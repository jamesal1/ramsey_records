## How it was found

Seed: the disjoint union of Van Overberghe's 64-vertex (K5-e, K7-e)-graph `J5J7n64` (VO64), the graph that proves R(K5-e, K7-e) = 65 and K4, a 68-vertex (K5-e, K8)-graph. The complement of a disjoint union is the join of the complements, so the largest clique (or clique minus an edge) in the complement of a union can be read off from the parts. Here the complement of VO64 has no K7, and K4's complement adds at most one vertex to a clique.

A *SAT step* is an exact search (CaDiCaL through PySAT) that deletes up to d vertices of the current graph and adds d + 1 new ones, keeping every other vertex and edge. Edges at the new vertices are the variables; every forbidden subgraph in a candidate is blocked by a new clause, and the search either returns a graph one vertex larger or proves that no graph of that form exists.

From the 68-vertex seed, three steps with d ≤ 2 gave 69, 70 and 71 vertices (on the VM). Only added vertices were ever deleted, so vertices 0–63 are VO64 unchanged.

Superseded by [the 72-vertex graph](K5e_K8_n72.md), one more step of the same ladder.
