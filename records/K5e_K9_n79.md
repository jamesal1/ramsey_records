## How it was found

Seed: the disjoint union of Van Overberghe's 64-vertex (K5-e, K7-e)-graph `J5J7n64` (VO64), the graph that proves R(K5-e, K7-e) = 65 and a 10-vertex graph with no K5-e whose complement is triangle-free, a 74-vertex (K5-e, K9)-graph that ties DS1.18's bound of 75. The complement of a disjoint union is the join of the complements, so the largest clique (or clique minus an edge) in the complement of a union can be read off from the parts.

A *SAT step* is an exact search (CaDiCaL through PySAT) that deletes up to d vertices of the current graph and adds d + 1 new ones, keeping every other vertex and edge. Edges at the new vertices are the variables; every forbidden subgraph in a candidate is blocked by a new clause, and the search either returns a graph one vertex larger or proves that no graph of that form exists.

Five steps with d ≤ 2 climbed from 74 to 79 vertices; vertices 0–63 are VO64 unchanged. On the 79-vertex graph the d ≤ 1 step and the d ≤ 2 step (23 min) are UNSAT; d ≤ 3 was stopped after 11.6 h. Because K10-e contains K9, the same graph proves R(K5-e, K10-e) ≥ 80.

Superseded the same day by [an 80-vertex Cayley graph](K5e_K9_n80.md).
